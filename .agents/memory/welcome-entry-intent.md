name: Welcome entry intent
description: The final welcome-screen specification supersedes the earlier timed splash.
---

The welcome screen waits for “Step in”; it does not automatically dismiss or skip on background clicks. Show it on a fresh app load only, not business switching or in-app navigation.

**Why:** The user's later exact design attachment replaced the earlier brief timed greeting with a deliberate entry action and staggered rise/fade animations.

**How to apply:** Preserve the specified cream-to-rose gradient, Fraunces headline/italic name, Inter eyebrow/link, and manual soft transition. Prefer a logged-in profile name when available, otherwise the stored display name; keep this addition separate from calendar and learning behavior.

The initial calendar post ripple must wait for entry, not run invisibly behind the welcome screen. It is a one-time left-to-right entrance, never a replay on completion, additions, or navigation.

**Why:** The user specifically wants the wave when the calendar first becomes visible; routine updates should stay calm and immediate.
