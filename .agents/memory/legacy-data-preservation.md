---
name: Legacy sample cleanup
description: Conservative boundary between removable samples and user-owned legacy work.
---

Treat an exact untouched auto-saved demo service as removable only when no saved business, post, catalog customization, or seasonal setting claims it. Preserve modified or ambiguous entries and keep their workspace reachable.

**Why:** Old versions persisted demo defaults without a provenance marker. The user requires removal of built-in samples without loss of user-created services or learning; broad deletion by business ID or familiar service name would erase real work.

**How to apply:** Keep legacy identities solely for recognizing and recovering old data, never as new-business defaults or calendar fixtures. Regression fixtures must explicitly create their businesses and services rather than relying on production sample seeding.

Automatically inherited global platform options are not evidence of a business-specific edit.

**Why:** Old versions copied the global platform catalog into every sample business on load. Treating those copies as customization restores all demo tabs for users who only added a global platform.

**How to apply:** Compare legacy catalogs against both built-in defaults and saved global options; still preserve business-specific platform selections, aliases, and other edits.
