---
name: Invisible flat-post ladder
description: Product rules for silently adapting suggestions and monthly pacing to a service's weak-view streak.
---

The user wants a per-service flat streak that never appears as a count, strike, badge or scoreboard. It only changes normal-looking suggestions: first weak result does nothing, second suggests reposting the same content (“worth another shot — timing may have been off”), third changes the angle or format while keeping the topic, fourth and onward reduces suggestions to about monthly without dropping the service.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested background logic, not another performance display.

Evaluate only after three posts with recorded views in the same business and service. Compare each eligible post with that service's average at that point in its chronological history, including the result itself. Views at or above average reset the streak; missing results and skipped posts are not evidence of failure.

Reconstruct the counter from saved results rather than incrementing it on every Save. Do not retroactively reclassify an earlier recovery against a later average.

**Why:** Resaving or editing a result must not manufacture extra failures, and reloads must preserve the policy without a second, potentially stale counter store.

**How to apply:** Keep the count out of all user-facing objects and text. Keep Add/Skip consent, preserve already-approved posts on recovery, resume ordinary suggestions after reset, and apply monthly pacing to conversion follow-up dates without replacing their trust-first sequence. A distant future planned post must not suppress the intervening monthly presence.
