---
name: Airtime balance intent
description: Why the at-a-glance panel uses monthly service airtime rather than aggregate results or weekly recommendations.
---

The user’s at-a-glance design calls for service airtime bars, not aggregate Views/Saves/Bookings boxes. Their requested baseline is the number of posts each service has in the selected month relative to other services, with an above-average-view boost and a visible minimum for sparse or underperforming services.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested replacing the results boxes with their airtime design. The separate weekly allocation model answers a different question: recommended capacity, rather than the calendar’s monthly mix.

**How to apply:** Preserve this meaning when changing the panel. Do not substitute seasonal weekly allocation scores for monthly post volume. Performance adjustments retain the existing three-measured-post gate.
