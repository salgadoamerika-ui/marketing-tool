---
name: Service and Campaign mode intent
description: User-defined offering lifecycles, public labels, and metadata-only scope of mode setup.
---

An ongoing offering is internally evergreen but must always be called “Service” in the interface. The word evergreen must never appear in user-visible copy, accessible names or labels. A time-bound offering is internally campaign and publicly “Campaign.”

**Why:** The user calls this a core product concept and explicitly requires plain-language public labels.

A Service has no end date and never ends: its purpose is sustained visibility and a consistent drip of bookings. A Campaign has a start and deadline, and its intended lifecycle builds toward the close and then finishes.

**Why:** These are the user's business definitions, not classifications inferred from post performance or content type.

**How to apply:** Mode setup is metadata only, not authorization to change suggestion behavior. Wait for the user's separate mode-specific behavior request before altering cadence, ramp-up, cutoff or recovery rules. Existing offerings default to Service rather than inventing campaign dates; users explicitly choose Campaign and its dates.
